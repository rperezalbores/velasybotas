Last Night in Thailand

Langkawi was two days away. Artemisa was provisioned, the paperwork was done, and the only responsible thing left to do was find a beach bar, order something cold, and spend the last Thai evening doing what Thailand does best — making it very difficult to leave.

Longtail boats at sunset, Southern Thailand
Longtail boats at sunset — Thailand's way of saying a proper goodbye

The coastline is pristine. Limestone karsts dropping into water the colour of a swimming pool, beaches lined up one after another with embarrassing generosity. No hotels looming over the sand, no cranes on the horizon. The sailing is easy — anchorages everywhere, holding good, winds predictable. Customs and immigration handled with efficiency and good humour. The pad thai alone justifies the detour.

We spent weeks threading through the islands south of Phuket, a new beach every day, something worth stopping for every evening. Artemisa will come back.
