## DAY 3 — Dubai to Maldives

**DATE:** 3/12/25 — **TIME:** 13:00 — N24°19' / E58°08' — **LOG: 1788nm**

We detected a fuel leak last evening on the starboard fuel tank. It is the connector fitting between the fuel tank and the engine fuel suction hose. We stopped the leak by switching to the other fuel tank (port) using the tank selector lever. We left the issue for this morning.

This morning after tea, around 11:00 we started cleaning. Using the manual vacuum oil extractor pump we sucked up around 20L!! of diesel that was around the fuel tank (most of it) and the bilge. That was a messy endeavor!!

While removing the fuel, a big bite on the fishing line! Quickly went to deck, put the reel in hard setting, felt the fish (it looked very big) and right after the fishing line snapped! Pity indeed but fortunate as it would have been difficult to deal with a sea monster.

After tea again, we successfully managed to pump fuel from the starboard bladder to the port tank. We used correct hoses with a drill pump. Clean job. In less than 10 minutes we filled up the tank.
**Back in business!!**

## DAY 5 — Dubai to Maldives

**DATE:** 5/12/25 — **TIME:** 8:00 — N23°35' / E58°37' — **LOG: 1897nm**

Leaving Al Bahar Marina after a rocky night where wind & tide kept us parallel to the waves.

Cleaned the bilge, solar panels, sealed hatches and go!!

## DAY 7 — Dubai to Maldives

**SUNDAY 7/12/25 — TIME:** 10:28 — N22°25' / E60°24' — **LOG: 2083nm** — 7.5kt true
**COG:** 120°

We are finally determined sailing towards our destination. Wind is not strong, quite weak in fact, making relatively slow progress. It is around 5kt — I guess we cannot complain. Very pleasant sailing however.

On the negative side we have 75% shade on our solar panels — sailing east the sun is in front of the sail casting shade on the aft of the boat. Bimini completely shaded and half the arch. Effectively we have only one solar panel working — we are energy deficient.

Charging from alternator has some issues as the Nordkyn controller cannot control the alternator for some reason — starter battery problem? We will use our generator — despite my hate, it is becoming useful.

I am almost certain that AIS is dead after the reverse polarity event — really unfortunate. Starlink working well.
