Sevilla at 5am smells of jasmine and possibility. By 9am it smells of sun and mistake. Leave early, stop early — the Vía de la Plata's first commandment.

The route out of the city follows the old Roman road. The Romans did not do anything without a reason. This road exists because it was the most direct path between the silver mines of the north and the port of Hispalis. You are walking the supply chain of an empire.

[PHOTO]

The first day is 23km. By km 18 you have recalibrated your understanding of what 23km means.
